****# ORIGIN_REGION_EXPERIENCE

## Status

`Frozen Experience Contract`

## Purpose

The `Origin Region` is the first place every `Traveler` experiences.

It establishes trust.

It teaches the language of the world without instructions.

## Arrival

The `Traveler` always arrives.

The world never pops into existence.

The place already exists before the Traveler reaches it.

## Scale

The `Origin Region` feels intimate.

The world beyond feels enormous.

The Traveler understands:

`This place belongs to something much larger.`

## Identity

The `Origin Region` is immediately recognizable.

It has:

- a center
- a boundary
- an atmosphere
- a landmark
- a path forward

## The Origin Monument

It is not decoration.

It exists because this place is the `Origin`.

Its presence should communicate stability.

It anchors the `Traveler`.

## The Traveler

The `Traveler` belongs here.

Spawn feels intentional.

Never random.

Never technical.

## Discovery

The `Traveler` immediately understands:

- where they are
- where they can go
- that there is more to discover

without reading text.
