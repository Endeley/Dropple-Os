# FIRST_WORLD_EXPERIENCE

## Status

`Frozen Experience Contract`

## Purpose

The `First World` is not a homepage.

It is not a dashboard.

It is not a landing page.

It is the first place a `Traveler` enters inside the `Dropple World`.

Every decision must reinforce one illusion:

`I have entered a living world.`

## Experience Goals

The browser disappears.

The world becomes the interface.

The user forgets they are inside a website.

The user feels present.

## Emotional Journey

`Arrival`

`↓`

`Orientation`

`↓`

`Curiosity`

`↓`

`Exploration`

`↓`

`Discovery`

## The World

The world is larger than what can currently be seen.

The visible world is only one part of a much larger continuous existence.

Nothing should imply the world ends at the screen edges.

## Movement

Nothing teleports.

Nothing behaves like webpages.

Everything exists continuously.

The world remains present.

## Interaction

The world invites curiosity.

It never overwhelms.

Nothing shouts for attention.

Discovery is rewarded.

## Visual Identity

Quiet.

Massive.

Timeless.

Living.

Purposeful.

Readable.

Never cluttered.

## Forbidden Feelings

The user must never feel:

- `I clicked another page.`
- `I opened another section.`
- `I changed screens.`

Instead they should feel:

- `I travelled.`
- `I arrived.`
- `I discovered.`
