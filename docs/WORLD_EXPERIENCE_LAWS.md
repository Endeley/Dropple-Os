# WORLD_EXPERIENCE_LAWS

## Status

`Frozen Experience Contract`

## Authority Level

`Highest Experience Authority`

## Purpose

Defines the immutable laws governing how `Dropple` must be perceived.

These laws may influence implementation.

They may never redefine constitutional truth.

All other experience contracts derive from these laws.

## Law 1 - Traveler, Not Visitor

The user is always a `Traveler`, never a visitor to a webpage.

## Law 2 - Persistent World

The world is always persistent, never reconstructed as pages.

## Law 3 - Travel, Not Navigation

Movement is experienced as travel, never navigation.

## Law 4 - Camera Support

The camera supports the `Traveler` and never replaces them.

## Law 5 - Regions Are Places

Every region is a real place with identity, scale, and purpose.

## Law 6 - Browser as Viewport

The browser is a viewport into the `Dropple World`, not the product itself.

## Law 7 - Presence Before Interface

The user should perceive the world before they perceive the interface.

### Explanation

The world is primary.

Interface exists to support the world.

Interface must never dominate the Traveler's perception.

Whenever possible, information should be communicated through the
environment rather than overlays, panels, or instructions.

## Standing Experience Gate

Every implementation should be judged against one question:

`Does this strengthen the illusion that the user exists inside one persistent living world?`
