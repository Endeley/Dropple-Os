## Experience Criteria

### 1. Intention Recognition

`PASS`

The plan begins from creator intent rather than product structure. It defines
Creative Initiation as the threshold between having intent and entering the
Living World with confidence, which is consistent with the frozen product
constitution.

### 2. Creative Direction

`PASS`

The plan clearly says the creator chooses a way to create rather than software,
tools, files, or editors. That preserves the constitutional rule that Creative
Initiation begins with creative language rather than technical configuration.

### 3. Confidence Before Entry

`PASS`

The chapter promise and success condition are explicit: the creator should
leave knowing exactly how they want to begin. That is precise enough for
implementation planning to target confidence rather than generic onboarding.

### 4. World Continuity

`PASS`

The boundary between Creative Initiation and Creative Arrival is clearly
defined. The plan states that Initiation ends at commitment and Arrival begins
when the Living World answers, "You belong here," which preserves threshold
continuity rather than implying an app-launch transition.

### 5. Language-First Experience

`PASS`

The plan keeps Blueprint and Template choice subordinate to creative language.
This matches the frozen constitutional laws that starting method follows
language and that Blueprints and Templates become relevant only after the
creator knows how they want to create.

## Verdict

`Accepted`

`CREATE_WORLD_STAGE1_CREATIVE_INITIATION_PLAN.md` has earned implementation
planning.

Implementation planning should now express the already-defined conversation. It
should not invent new product truths about intent, language choice, starting
method, or the boundary into Creative Arrival.
