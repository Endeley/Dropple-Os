# Create World Creative Initiation Integration Plan

## Purpose

This document defines how the `Creative Initiation` place and the
`Creative Initiation` conversation become one coherent creator experience.

It is not an implementation-detail plan.

It exists because two planning tracks are now complete:

- the **Conversation Track** defines what Dropple and the creator need to
  understand together
- the **Environment Track** defines what kind of place `Creative Initiation`
  must be

This artifact answers the bridge question:

`How does the place naturally express the conversation so that the creator experiences one seamless Creative Initiation?`

## Integration Question

How should `Creative Initiation` behave so that:

- the place makes the conversation feel natural
- the conversation reveals the identity of the place
- both together guide the creator toward commitment
- the threshold into `Creative Arrival` feels like one continuous world transition

## Integration Rule

This plan may define how place and conversation meet.

It may not:

- redefine place identity
- redefine the conversation sequence
- prescribe route or shell ownership
- prescribe component structure
- invent implementation mechanics

Those belong to other artifacts or later work.

## 1. The Place Hosts the Conversation

`Creative Initiation` must feel like the natural home of the opening
conversation.

That means the place should not merely contain prompts layered on top of an
unrelated environment.

Instead:

- the place should feel purpose-built for reflection and direction
- the conversation should feel native to the place
- nothing in the environment should suggest that authoring has already begun

The creator should experience:

`This place exists for this conversation.`

not:

`A conversation has been temporarily overlaid onto an editor.`

## 2. The Conversation Reveals the Place

The environment should not need to explain its own identity through separate
instructional framing.

The conversation itself should make the identity of the place legible.

As the creator moves through the conversation, they should naturally understand:

- why they are here
- what the place is helping them do
- why creation has not yet begun
- why commitment matters before entry into the Living World

The conversation therefore does two jobs at once:

- it clarifies creator direction
- it reveals the meaning of the place

## 3. The Environment Evolves as Understanding Grows

`Creative Initiation` should not feel static.

As intention becomes clearer, the environment should feel more resolved and
more directional.

This does not imply any specific animation or UI mechanism.

It means the integration must preserve a progression such as:

- uncertainty becomes orientation
- orientation becomes direction
- direction becomes readiness

The place should therefore feel like it is responding to growing understanding,
not waiting passively for a button press.

## 4. The Threshold Into Creative Arrival

The threshold into `Creative Arrival` is the most important integration point.

The creator should feel:

- the place has fulfilled its purpose
- commitment has meaning
- the world is now responding
- creation is about to begin

The creator should not feel:

- a wizard completed
- an editor unlocked
- another screen loaded
- a modal dismissed

The integrated experience must therefore preserve this handoff law:

`The place completes its purpose, and the world answers.`

## 5. Implementation Boundaries

The following boundaries must remain intact during later implementation:

- the conversation never owns runtime truth
- the environment never owns creator truth
- runtime never defines place identity
- creative language never dominates before commitment
- implementation may express the threshold, but it may not redefine what the threshold means

These are the guardrails that keep integration lawful.

## Integration Targets

Future implementation planning should preserve these experiential targets:

1. The creator always knows they are in the beginning place of creation.
2. The environment and conversation never contradict each other.
3. The environment becomes more directional as creator understanding grows.
4. Commitment feels meaningful.
5. The transition into `Creative Arrival` feels like one continuous world response.

## Out of Scope

This document does not define:

- exact screen structure
- visual layout
- interaction timing
- shell visibility rules
- animation choreography
- route transitions
- CI-1 code changes
- CI-2, CI-3, or CI-4 implementation details

Those belong to later implementation-planning or slice-level work.

## Success Condition

This integration plan is complete when future work can answer:

`How do place and conversation work together as one seamless Creative Initiation experience?`

without reopening either:

- the identity of the place
- the meaning of the conversation

At that point, implementation planning may begin.
