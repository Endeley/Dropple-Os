## Preamble

Dropple exists as a lawful creative operating system.

Truth is deterministic.  
Generation is bounded by law.  
Derivation may enrich authority but may never replace authority.

Dropple evolves through:

- seeds
- environments
- tools
- creative physics

while preserving one constitutional mutation truth.

## Article I — Sovereign Truth

### Law 1 — Event Sovereignty

Events are the sole mutation truth.

No generated system may bypass dispatcher-owned event mutation.

### Law 2 — Replay Sovereignty

All truth must remain replayable and deterministic.

Generated tools, seeds, and physics evaluators must replay identically.

### Law 3 — Projection Sovereignty

UI consumes projections.  
UI does not own truth.

No surface may create parallel authority.

## Article II — Structural Order

### Law 4 — Constitutional Layer Order

Dropple’s active constitutional layer axis remains:

`core -> infrastructure -> runtime -> workspace -> ui -> product`

Lower layers may never import upward.

This law remains subordinate to `docs/LAW.md` until amended there directly.

Engine systems may exist as subsystems, but are not yet a separate constitutional layer.

### Law 5 — Three-Layer Workspace Law

Platform truth executes.  
Workspace truth classifies.  
Mode truth exposes.

Execution may not leak into taxonomy.

### Law 6 — Fixed Constitutional Workspaces

Five workspaces are constitutional:

- `design`
- `media`
- `build`
- `system`
- `collaborate`

No generated system may create a sovereign sixth workspace.

### Law 7 — Fifteen Canonical Modes

The proposed canonical 15 are:

### Design
- `uiux`
- `graphic`
- `document`

### Media
- `animation`
- `video`
- `audio`

### Build
- `application`
- `logic`
- `automation`

### System
- `tokens`
- `components`
- `governance`

### Collaborate
- `review`
- `knowledge`
- `production`

Derived surfaces may exist.  
Canonical modes remain finite.

This law is not active until the `25 -> 15` mode amendment is completed.

### Law 8 — Overlay Preservation Law

When a sovereign mode is collapsed, its payload contracts must survive through explicit overlay ownership.

Overlays may expose capability surfaces, but do not constitute sovereign modes.

No sovereign mode may be collapsed unless all owned payloads are explicitly rehomed.

## Article III — Seed Law

### Law 9 — Certified Seed Authority

All generated creative evolution begins as certified seeds.

Seeds must be:

- compiled
- certified
- versioned
- replayable

### Law 10 — Lineage Law

Seeds may:

- fork
- derive
- merge
- specialize

through lawful ancestry graphs only.

No cyclic lineage.

### Law 11 — Derived Systems Law

Generated systems may enrich authority, but may never become authority.

## Article IV — Environment Law

### Law 12 — Derived Environment Law

Seeds may generate bounded environment overlays:

- capability overlays
- panel overlays
- tool overlays

They may not:

- alter runtime truth
- inject reducers
- redefine dispatcher law

### Law 13 — Activation Law

Canonical workspace activation resolves first.

Seed-derived overlays may enrich activation second.

Never reverse that order.

## Article V — Tool Law

### Law 14 — Tool Synthesis Law

Generated tools may exist only as interpreted bounded specifications.

Never arbitrary executable code injection.

### Law 15 — Recursive Tool Law

Generated tools may recurse only through:

- approved handler families
- interpreted bounded specs

Recursion must remain:

- capability-gated
- intent-lawful
- subordinate to existing mutation authority

Recursive derivation is allowed.  
Recursive sovereignty is forbidden.

## Article VI — Creative Physics Law

### Law 16 — Creative Physics Law

Creative physics evaluators may produce derived behavior:

- constraints
- forces
- emergence
- procedural motion

Physics may influence evaluation.

Physics may not mutate durable truth.

## Article VII — Knowledge Law

### Law 17 — Generated Knowledge Law

Generated knowledge structures may assist creation, but may never become mutation authority.

This includes:

- knowledge graphs
- semantic assistants
- design reasoning engines
- creative memory systems

## Article VIII — Species Law

### Law 18 — Species Law

Seeds may evolve into families, species, and ecosystems, provided lineage legality is preserved.

## Article IX — Self-Extension Law

### Law 19 — Open-Ended Lawful Evolution

Dropple is open-ended, not unbounded.

Infinite derivation.  
Finite axioms.
