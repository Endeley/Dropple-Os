## Structural Participants Under Authority

The structural participants inherited from `ARR2.1 Structure` are:

- `World Truth`
- `Traveler`
- `Revelation`
- `Representation Input Surface`

No additional participants are introduced here.

## Ownership

### 1. World Truth Ownership

`World Truth` is the sole constitutional authority over:

- existence
- identity
- geography
- relationships
- semantics
- state

`World Truth` remains the only participant that may define what exists.

### 2. Traveler Ownership

`Traveler` is the sole constitutional authority over traveler-relative
knowledge condition.

This includes the traveler-side condition required for truth to become
knowable, but not world truth itself.

### 3. Revelation Ownership

`Revelation` is the sole constitutional authority over:

- knowability
- lawful revelation triggers
- revelation progression
- revelation persistence
- rediscovery state
- the mediation of knowability between traveler and world truth

`Revelation` owns only what becomes knowable and under what lawful
conditions.

It does not own the truth that becomes knowable.

### 4. Representation Input Surface Ownership

`Representation Input Surface` is the sole constitutional authority over the
downstream intake of revealed knowledge for future expression.

It may receive what `Revelation` has made knowable.

It does not own revelation decisions.

## Decision Boundaries

### World Truth May

- define what exists
- define world semantics
- define world relationships
- define world state

### World Truth Must Never

- decide what is knowable to a traveler
- decide revelation progression
- decide revelation persistence
- decide representation of revealed knowledge

### Traveler May

- stand as the subject relative to whom knowledge may or may not be knowable
- carry traveler-relative condition relevant to revelation

### Traveler Must Never

- create world truth
- modify world truth
- declare truth knowable independently of revelation
- express revealed knowledge as representation

### Revelation May

- determine whether existing world truth is knowable to a traveler
- govern the lawful progression of knowability
- govern whether knowability persists
- distinguish first revelation from rediscovery

### Revelation Must Never

- create world truth
- modify world truth
- redefine world semantics
- redefine traveler identity
- express revealed knowledge
- decide how revealed knowledge is represented

### Representation Input Surface May

- receive only what revelation has made knowable
- serve as the downstream constitutional boundary for future expression

### Representation Input Surface Must Never

- decide what is knowable
- alter revealed knowledge
- modify world truth
- modify revelation state

## Information Authority

### Information Origin

`World Truth` originates:

- entities
- identity
- geography
- relationships
- semantics
- state

`Traveler` originates:

- traveler-relative knowledge condition

`Revelation` originates:

- knowability state
- revelation progression state
- revelation persistence state
- rediscovery state

### Information Consumption

`Revelation` may consume:

- existing world truth from `World Truth`
- traveler-relative condition from `Traveler`

`Representation Input Surface` may consume:

- only what `Revelation` has made knowable

### Information Observation

The `Traveler` may observe only what is knowable through `Revelation`.

The `Representation Input Surface` may observe only revealed knowledge, not
unrevealed world truth.

### Information Prohibitions

`Revelation` may not originate world truth.

`Representation Input Surface` may not originate revelation state.

`Traveler` may not originate world semantics.

`World Truth` may not originate knowability state.

## Constitutional Contracts

### Contract 1 - World Truth Supremacy

All revelation remains subordinate to `World Truth`.

If revelation and world truth appear to conflict, `World Truth` is
authoritative.

### Contract 2 - Traveler Relationality

Knowability is always traveler-relative.

No participant may redefine revelation as a property of world truth alone.

### Contract 3 - Revelation Non-Creation

`Revelation` may govern access only.

It may never create, replace, reinterpret, or modify the truth it reveals.

### Contract 4 - Downstream Expression Constraint

Nothing may be represented unless `Revelation` has made it knowable.

Representation may only consume revealed knowledge.

### Contract 5 - Boundary Integrity

Each participant must remain within its structural responsibility:

- `World Truth` answers `what exists`
- `Traveler` carries traveler-relative knowledge condition
- `Revelation` answers `what is knowable`
- `Representation Input Surface` receives what may later be expressed

Crossing these boundaries is a constitutional violation.

## Authority Summary

The constitutional authority model for `Revelation` is:

- `World Truth` owns truth
- `Traveler` owns traveler-relative condition
- `Revelation` owns knowability
- `Representation Input Surface` owns downstream intake of revealed knowledge

This preserves the full constitutional chain:

`Truth -> Behavior -> Structure -> Authority`

without introducing implementation concerns.
