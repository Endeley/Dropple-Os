# CREATE WORLD — STAGE 1: CREATIVE INITIATION

## Purpose

Creative Initiation is the creator's first conversation with Dropple.

Its purpose is to help the creator confidently choose a way to create before entering the Living World.

Dropple does not begin with creation. It begins with intention.

The creator never chooses software. The creator chooses a way to create.

## Artifact

Authority for this Product Chapter:

`docs/CREATE_WORLD_STAGE1_CREATIVE_INITIATION_PLAN.md`

## Chapter

Creative Initiation

## Question

How should the first conversation between Dropple and its creator unfold?

## Promise

Every creator should leave Creative Initiation feeling confident about how they are going to begin.

## Success Condition

Creative Initiation is complete when the creator can honestly say:

"I know exactly how I want to begin."

At that point:

- Creative Initiation ends.
- Creative Arrival begins.

## Creator Progression

Creative Initiation should guide the creator through this progression:

1. I have something I want to create.
2. I recognize the creative language that best expresses it.
3. I understand how I want to begin.
4. I commit to that beginning.
5. I enter the Living World.

## What Belongs Here

- Creative intent
- Creative language recognition
- Starting-method confidence
- Blueprint or Template path selection when appropriate
- Commitment to begin

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Project management
- File management
- Editor chrome
- Canvas implementation
- Runtime concepts
- Workspace configuration
- Advanced settings
- Template or Blueprint implementation details

## Product Truth

Creative Initiation is not a launcher, dashboard, or setup wizard.

It is the threshold between:

- having intent
- and entering a living creative world with confidence

The creator should never feel they are choosing an application.

They should feel Dropple understands what they want to create and is helping them begin in the right way.

## Boundary

Creative Initiation ends the moment the creator has committed to a way of beginning.

Creative Arrival begins the moment the Living World receives that commitment and answers:

"You belong here."
