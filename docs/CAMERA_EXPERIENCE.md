# CAMERA_EXPERIENCE

## Status

`Frozen Experience Contract`

## Purpose

The camera is not the `Traveler`.

The camera is the Traveler's companion.

## Camera Philosophy

The camera observes.

The `Traveler` acts.

The camera supports.

The camera never becomes the focus.

## Default Position

Slightly elevated.

Slightly behind.

Always preserving:

- orientation
- destination
- surroundings

## Arrival Camera

Reveal the place.

Reveal scale.

Reveal the `Traveler`.

Reveal the path.

Never rush.

## Exploration Camera

Maintain direction.

Maintain comfort.

Never fight movement.

Never lose the `Traveler`.

## Discovery Camera

Small adjustments.

Never cinematic.

Never dramatic.

Only enough to help the `Traveler` notice something meaningful.

## Camera Rules

Never clip through landmarks.

Never lose orientation.

Never hide the destination.

Never induce motion sickness.

Never feel mechanical.

## Feeling

The camera should feel invisible.

When people remember using `Dropple`,

they should remember the world,

not the camera.
