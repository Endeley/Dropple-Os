## Purpose

`ARR2.1` existed to define `Revelation` as a constitutional subsystem of the
`Dropple World Pipeline`.

Its purpose was not to implement discovery, UI, or representation.

Its purpose was to establish the constitutional truth, behavior, structure,
and authority of `Revelation` so future implementation can build against a
stable boundary instead of inventing one.

## Constitutional Outcome

### Dropple World Pipeline

`World`  
Authority: `What exists.`

`Projection`  
Authority: `Where it is from the current viewpoint.`

`Revelation`  
Authority: `What is currently knowable.`

`Representation`  
Authority: `How revealed knowledge is expressed.`

### ARR2.1 Frozen Boundary

`ARR2.1` concludes with:

`World -> Projection -> Revelation -> Representation`

and establishes `Revelation` as the constitutional boundary between:

- existing `World` truth
- traveler-relative knowability
- downstream `Representation`

## Final Constitutional Result

`ARR2.1` establishes `Revelation` as a first-class constitutional subsystem.
