# TRAVEL_EXPERIENCE

## Status

`Frozen Experience Contract`

## Purpose

Travel is the primary way people experience `Dropple`.

Travel is never navigation.

## Travel Philosophy

Movement is continuous.

Distance exists.

Orientation exists.

Regions exist.

Arrival matters.

## Travel

The `Traveler` moves.

The `World` remains.

The `Camera` follows.

## Travel Must Feel

Natural.

Grounded.

Purposeful.

Continuous.

Never rushed.

## Departure

The `Traveler` understands what they are leaving.

## Journey

Movement has duration.

Distance is perceivable.

Landmarks help orientation.

## Arrival

Arrival should feel rewarding.

Not dramatic.

Simply meaningful.

## Forbidden

No page transitions.

No fake loading screens.

No teleporting.

No webpage metaphors.

No tab switching.

Travel always happens inside one continuous world.
