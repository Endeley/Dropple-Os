## Review Criteria

### 1. Structural Fidelity

`PASS`

The implementation plan introduces structural regions that derive directly from
the frozen behavior plan:

- `World Identity Region`
- `Creative Language Discovery Region`
- `Recognition Region`
- `Continuity Support Region`
- `Transition Region`

These regions are not invented features.

They are structural translations of already-frozen behavioral
responsibilities:

- establish where the creator is
- reveal the existence of Creative Languages
- support recognition of belonging
- support lawful continuity
- prepare the threshold into language-specific `Creative Initiation`

No structurally dominant region has been added that would redefine the place.

### 2. Behavioral Fidelity

`PASS`

The implementation plan preserves the complete experience-state model from the
behavior plan:

- `Arrival`
- `Orientation`
- `Discovery`
- `Recognition`
- `Commitment`
- `Exit`

It also preserves the non-rigid nature of those states by explicitly allowing
adaptation for:

- new creators
- returning creators
- continuity-driven creators

Nothing in the structural translation collapses the `First World` into a fixed
wizard or stepper.

The creator's behavioral journey remains intact.

### 3. Presentation Neutrality

`PASS`

The implementation plan remains independent of specific visual expression.

It does not commit the product to:

- cards
- grids
- sidebars
- headers
- navigation bars
- particular interaction patterns

Instead, it defines presentation constraints at the level of what must remain
true:

- identity before utility
- discovery before direction
- belonging before configuration
- continuity without dominance
- same world, different threshold

That preserves freedom for later presentation and UI work.

### 4. Truth Preservation

`PASS`

Nothing in the implementation plan changes the original constitutional truth
of the `First World`.

It remains:

- the entrance to the Living World
- orientation before creation
- discovery before direction

It also preserves the critical boundary that:

- the `First World` helps creators discover where they belong
- language-specific `Creative Initiation` helps them decide how to begin

That constitutional separation remains fully intact.

### 5. Implementation Guardrail Integrity

`PASS`

The implementation plan gives engineers clear freedom within clear limits.

Implementation may choose:

- components
- layout patterns
- navigation patterns
- interaction affordances
- adaptive presentation for different creator contexts

Implementation may not alter:

- place identity
- departure condition
- the boundary between `First World` and `Creative Initiation`
- the dominance hierarchy of orientation over project management

That is the correct level of guardrail.

It allows multiple implementations while preserving one product.

`DROPPLE_FIRST_WORLD_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` translates the frozen truth and
